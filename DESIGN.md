# Design System: SlipStream

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Lit Console"**

SlipStream is a console lit by the Work it is showing. A Work's artwork sits blurred behind the page, and its hue runs through every surface and every ink, so a page about *Dune: Part Two* is a page in Dune's colour. Everything else holds still against that light. Red and amber are fixed alarms, green is a fixed all-clear, and live progress is white with a glow in the Work's hue. Glass blur belongs only to things that float. Lists, tables and cards are tinted translucent sheets with hairline edges, and nothing is drawn as a heavy box.

There is one theme, and it is dark. The Admin console is dense, built from A's layout in the direction ticket: a collapsible sidebar with live counts, a top bar with a ⌘K palette, and pages that open on a status sentence. The portal shares every token and component but runs at the Touch density, with B's poster-led layout and no machinery on screen. Geist sets everything. Geist Mono appears only in single values someone copies or compares down a column.

Motion is slow where light moves and quick where controls answer. The tint crossfades over 1.1s when focus moves to another Work. Buttons, switches and menus respond in about 200ms. Sheets and dialogs arrive on a spring.

**Key Characteristics:**
- The Work in focus colours the page through a blurred ambient layer and two CSS properties, a hue and a saturation.
- Fixed signal hues: red for failure, amber for warning, green for a passed check. None of them ever takes the tint.
- Live is luminance. A running job is white with the Work's glow, and Met is solid white.
- Translucent tinted surfaces, with glass blur only on floating chrome and overlays.
- Two densities: Console for the Admin desktop shell, Touch for the Admin phone shell and the whole portal.
- WCAG 2.2 AA is checked for every tint, and an ambient ceiling holds it.

## Colors

The palette is a tinted graphite whose hue follows the Work in focus, under three fixed signal hues and one solid white.

Every `hsl(214 …)` value in the frontmatter is the neutral resolution of a formula. Two registered CSS properties drive the tint: `--h` (the Work's hue, 0 to 360) and `--s` (its saturation, 18% to 70%). The frontmatter's `ambient.neutral` is their resting value. Inks keep their own saturation and take only the hue. Surfaces scale the ambient saturation. The canonical formulas follow, one row per tinted token, and `.impeccable/design.json` repeats them per token. The fixed signal colours (`fail*`, `warn*`, `ok*`, `solid`, `on-solid`) have no formula. `go run ./cmd/release gen tokens` writes the web theme from the frontmatter and this table, and `release lint` refuses a formula whose neutral resolution differs from its frontmatter value.

### Primary

- **Lit Graphite** (`page`, `glass`, `glass-raised`, `chrome`): The ground and the sheets laid on it. `page` paints under the ambient. `glass` carries tables, lists, the matrix and every card that is not a signal. `glass-raised` lifts a thing inside glass, such as a poster placeholder or a step disc. `chrome` is the sidebar and the bars.
- **Work Glow** (`glow`, `halo`, `live-fill`): The Work's own colour at full voice, and the only place it is bright. `glow` is the halo on live progress and the caret. `halo` is the outer focus ring. `live-fill` is the body of a chip that names something in flight.

### Secondary

- **Signal Red** (`fail` and its family): Failure only. `fail` is the solid chip, button, dot and failed stage segment, with `fail-deep` ink on it. `fail-card` is the near-opaque failure card, with `fail-text` for its copy, `fail-dim` for its secondary copy and `fail-ink` for its labels and for failure text set on glass. `fail-line` is the card's edge.
- **Signal Amber** (`warn` and its family): Warnings only, built the same way as red. `warn-line` is the one amber hairline on warning lines and amber rows.

### Tertiary

- **Signal Green** (`ok`, `ok-deep`): A check that passed, or a thing that is healthy. Examples are a Node online, a route hop passing, a provider key accepted, "Checked by Plex", Granted, a guardrail that allows a drop, Test connection passing and a seeding goal met. Green appears as a solid chip with `ok-deep` ink, a dot, or a check icon, always beside its words.

### Neutral

- **Ink tiers** (`ink-1` to `ink-4`): `ink-1` for primary text and values, `ink-2` for secondary text and unselected controls, `ink-3` for meta, counts and column headers. `ink-4` is not for text. It marks separators, chevrons, idle dots and disabled glyphs.
- **Fills and edges** (`fill`, `fill-hover`, `selected`, `hairline`, `hairline-strong`, `well`): Controls sit on `fill`. `selected` marks the chosen segment. Hairlines are 1px inset rings, never borders that change layout. `well` is the dark inset under inputs, code and attempt lists.
- **Solid White** (`solid`, `on-solid`): The primary action, Met, the toast and count badges. Dark `on-solid` ink always sits on it.

### The ambient

The ambient is a fixed layer behind each page, made of the Work's backdrop and a tint wash. The backdrop is blurred with `blur(64px) saturate(1.8)` and crossfades between two layers. The wash is two radial gradients at the Work's hue (26% and 22% lightness, alpha .55 and .45) over a vertical darkening at 4% to 5% lightness.

A sampler draws the poster onto a 24 × 36 canvas. It skips pixels with less than 4% chroma and weights each pixel by `sat² × (1 − |l − .5| × 1.6)`, which favours saturated mid-tones. It takes the circular mean of the hues, and sets `--s` to the weighted mean saturation × 80, clamped to 18% to 70%. A poster with no colourful pixels gives 220 / 12%.

Who sets the ambient:
- **A Work's page.** That Work.
- **Admin Home.** The most important job, meaning the job closest to Available, with a Member's request breaking ties. The pipeline sorts in that order, so the top row and the tint agree. In a desktop browser, hovering or focusing a pipeline row moves the tint to its Work after 90ms, and leaving returns it after 450ms. On phone, the topmost job in the middle band of the viewport sets it.
- **Portal Home.** The poster under the pointer or focus in a desktop browser, with the rest of the wall dimming. At rest, the first Work of the first shelf.
- **Any screen with no Work.** Nodes, Remote access, Settings, Providers and System use the neutral, hue 214 at 14%.
- **A failed Work never drives the tint.** Failure cards and rows carry no Work for the ambient.

### Named Rules

**The Ambient Ceiling Rule.** After its layers composite, the brightest region of the ambient stays at or below relative luminance 0.053. Text sits directly on the ambient (the status sentence, breadcrumbs, section counts), and 0.053 is the brightest ground on which `ink-3` still clears 4.5:1 at every hue. Before showing a backdrop, measure its brightest region in the 24 × 36 sample and set the image's `brightness()` to hold the ceiling. A fixed `brightness(.6)`, as the prototypes used, does not hold it: a white backdrop under that filter composites to about 0.16.

**The Fixed Signal Rule.** Red, amber and green never take the tint, and the tint never takes their meaning. A signal is a solid chip, a dot, a solid card (red and amber only) or an icon. Never a thin coloured word on a tinted ground.

**The Luminance Is Live Rule.** Running work is white with the Work's glow. Met is solid white. A hue on screen means trouble or a passed check, never motion.

**The Never Alone Rule.** Every signal colour comes with an icon and a word. Red and green are the pair most often confused, so colour alone never carries the state.

**The Contrast Proof Rule.** Change a colour token only if the contrast check in `go run ./cmd/release lint` still passes. It sweeps 120 hues at six saturations, finds the ambient ceiling, requires it to equal the frontmatter's `ambient.ceiling`, and checks 32 ink and surface pairs at it. It reads the same parsed tokens the theme is generated from, and its pairs, named by token, live in `cmd/release/internal/tokens/contrast.go`.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geist (with Helvetica Neue, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Geist (with Helvetica Neue, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono (with ui-monospace, monospace), for data only

**Character:** One grotesque at every size, tightened as it grows. Display and titles run at 700 with negative tracking. Body runs at 400 to 600 with none. Mono is a tool for values, not a voice.

### Hierarchy

The scale has ten sizes: 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 17, 20, 24, 34 and 56px. Geist and Geist Mono ship as bundled variable fonts (`@fontsource-variable/geist` and `@fontsource-variable/geist-mono`), and nothing loads a font from elsewhere. Use only the weights 400, 500, 600 and 700, never an in-between weight such as 650. `release gen tokens` refuses any other weight in the frontmatter.

- **Display** (700, 56px, 1.02): The portal's search hero headline and a Work's logo fallback on the portal.
- **Title large** (700, 34px, 1.15): The phone shell's large title.
- **Numeral** (600, 34px, 1, tabular): Big ordinals and headline figures, such as a provider's rank. Sans, never Mono.
- **Sentence** (600, 24px, 1.25): The desktop status sentence that opens Home, Torrents, Conversions and Remote access, and a page's title in the desktop shell. Also the portal's shelf titles and the wordmark on sign-in and setup.
- **Title** (700, 20px, 1.2): Phone section titles, the inspector title, dialog titles (a sheet's title too, since a Dialog becomes a sheet on phone) and the title of a sign-in or setup card.
- **Sentence touch** (600, 17px, 1.35): The phone status sentence, the phone nav bar's title, sheet items and a recommendation card's title.
- **Card title** (600, 15px, 1.3): A failure card's subject and a list row's title in Touch.
- **Body touch** (400, 15px, 1.45): Body copy at the Touch density.
- **Input touch** (400, 17px, 1.3): Text in Touch inputs. iOS zooms the page into any input set below 16px.
- **Heading** (600, 14px, 1.35): Section and panel headings in Console, and matrix row and column heads.
- **Body** (400, 13px, 1.45): Console body, table cells and buttons (600). Prose runs to 64ch at most.
- **Button** (600, 13px, 1): Button labels in Console. Touch buttons use Body touch at 600.
- **Meta** (400, 12px, 1.4): Secondary lines under titles, timestamps in prose and legends.
- **Chip** (600, 12px): Chips and badges. Signal chips are 700.
- **Label** (500, 11px, 1.3): Column headers, group labels, nav counts and the phone tab bar's labels.
- **Label caps** (600, 11px, 0.06em, uppercase): Only the key column of a key and value list on a failure card. No other text is uppercase.
- **Data** (Geist Mono 400, 0.92em of its context, tabular): Values under the Copy or Compare Rule.

### Named Rules

**The Copy or Compare Rule.** Geist Mono sets a single value that someone copies or compares down a column: rates, sizes, durations, ETAs, percentages, timestamps in tables, release names, file names, file specs, paths, hashes, keys, addresses and ports. Everything else is Geist. Counts inside sentences, section counts, ordinals, step numbers and weekday labels are Geist with tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). Apply Mono to the value's own element, never to a container that holds words.

**The Log Row Exception.** A line in a Node's Logs section sets whole in Mono: its time, level chip, subsystem and message. The Admin reads a log down its columns like a table of values, so the row is the one container that sets in Mono. Its attributes, shown when the row expands, stay in Mono too.

**The Names Are Words Rule.** Names are never Mono. A Work, a Node, a Member, a Profile and a Target are names someone gave a thing, so they read as words, even when a Node's name looks like a hostname.

## Layout

The system has two shells and two densities. The shell follows the viewport and the density follows the audience.

### Shells

- **Desktop shell (768px and up).** Three columns: the sidebar, the content under its top bar, and the Inspector's column when one is open.
  - **Sidebar.** 228px wide, collapsing to a 60px rail. It collapses on its own below 1100px, and the Admin can collapse it at any width with its button or ⌘K "Toggle sidebar". The choice is remembered per browser and holds at 1100px and up. Expanding the rail below 1100px is not remembered. The sidebar is `chrome` with a hairline on its right edge, and the mark (24px) and wordmark (17px) sit at its top beside the collapse button.
  - **Nav.** Items are 32px tall with 16px icons, `ink-2` until hovered (`fill`), and `selected` with `ink-1` at 500 when current. Activity carries a live count (a white glowing dot and a number), and other rows carry plain counts. A count shows only above zero and can carry an amber or red dot. Content Type rows nest under Library, indented 32px. The system rows, such as Nodes, sit below a separator. A row that lands on a page below its own, as Nodes lands on the Node the browser talks to, stays current on every page under it. On the rail, each row is its icon alone, with a Tooltip on the right carrying the label and count, and the live dot pinned to the icon's top right.
  - **Account.** The sidebar's foot is a 28px avatar with the Account's initial, the display name and "Admin". It opens a menu with Open the portal, the Startup sequence switch, Restart SlipStream… and Sign out.
  - **Top bar.** Sticky, 56px tall, laid out as `1fr minmax(320px, 440px) 1fr` with 28px sides. Breadcrumbs sit on the left in `ink-3`, with the current page in `ink-1` at 500. The ⌘K search capsule ("Search Works or run a command", with the platform's shortcut) sits in the middle. The right holds slots in a fixed order: the live connection line, shown only while reconnecting, then later controls such as live rates and the bell, then the page's main action.
  - **Content.** Padding is 24px top, 28px sides and 120px bottom, with a 1480px maximum width and 24px between sections. The page's title opens the content in Sentence type, 24px above what follows.
  - **Narrow desktop windows.** At 1360px and below, tables tighten their columns, and the legend, the status sentence's right-hand totals and the top bar's rates hide.
- **Phone shell (below 768px).** There is no top bar. A top-level page opens on a large-title row (the 34px title, then search and the avatar, which opens More). Once the title scrolls under the 44px nav bar, the bar turns glass and takes the title in Sentence touch. Sections sit 28px apart with 20px titles, and content keeps 16px gutters.
  - **Admin tab bar.** Glass, fixed to the bottom, with 49px tabs of a 24px icon over a Label. The current tab is `glow` and the rest are `ink-3`. The tabs are the nav's tab rows (Library, then Activity) followed by Search, which opens the palette, and More. Home takes the first tab when its page lands. A count shows as an 18px solid white badge. A tab keeps its last page and scroll position when switched away and back.
  - **More.** An action sheet over the current page with the rest of the nav and its counts, the account (name, Open the portal, Restart SlipStream… and Sign out), the Startup sequence switch, the version (`SlipStream <version>`) and Cancel. More is never the current tab.
  - **Portal tab bar.** Home, Search, Requests and Account.
  - **Navigation.** A page that is not a nav destination is pushed. Its nav bar is glass from the start, with a back link in `glow` labelled with the parent page's title, and the link goes to the parent rather than back in history. A nav row's landing page is that destination's top page and takes the row's label, so a Node's page is titled Nodes and its pushed sections link back to Nodes. Every menu becomes an action sheet, and every Dialog becomes a bottom sheet.
- **Safe areas.** Bars and sheets pad with `env(safe-area-inset-*)`, and the viewport meta carries `viewport-fit=cover`. Never hard-code the status bar or home indicator height.
- **Portal in a desktop browser.** A 64px top nav that sits transparent over the hero and turns to glass after 24px of scroll. It holds the wordmark (19px), the four destinations as 40px capsule links in Body touch with `selected` on the current one, and the avatar menu. The gutter is `clamp(24px, 3.6vw, 56px)`, and pages run full bleed.
- **Sign-in, setup and the Worker page.** No shell. A 400px glass card (radius 14, 24px padding, 20px in Touch) sits centred on the neutral ambient under the wordmark, and its title is Title.

### Densities

The shell sets the density on the root as `data-density`, and components read its variables, so one component serves both densities. The portal is Touch on every device. Every other page follows the shell.

- **Console.** The Admin desktop shell. 13px body, 32px buttons, icon buttons and inputs (28px small buttons), 44px single-line rows, 60px two-line rows and 36px menu items.
- **Touch.** The Admin phone shell and the whole portal on any device. 15px body, 44px minimum targets, 50px primary buttons, 48px inputs, 54px single-line list rows, 64px two-line rows and 52px menu and sheet items.

### Rhythm

Spacing runs on a 4px grid (2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32), the same as Tailwind's default `--spacing`. Cards pad 16px. List rows pad 16px at the sides and take their height from the density. Sections sit 24px apart (28px on a Work page and on phone).

### Named Rules

**The Container Rule.** A component reflows by container queries, not by viewport breakpoints. The Torrents table drops to four columns at 980px and becomes stacked cards at 620px, wherever it is drawn. An open Inspector narrows the page, and the table answers without a breakpoint of its own.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from translucency over the ambient, not from shadows. Surfaces at rest are flat tinted sheets edged by a 1px inset hairline. Shadows appear only under things that float, under posters, and under signal cards so they lift off the tint.

### Materials

- **Tinted translucent, no blur.** The sidebar (`chrome`), tables, lists, matrix, files, rails and board panels (`glass`), and things raised inside them (`glass-raised`).
- **Glass, blurred.** Only things that float.
  - The top bar, the phone tab bar, the nav bar and the status bar use `blur(24px) saturate(1.6)` over `chrome`. So does the portal's top nav once it turns to glass.
  - Menus, popovers, tooltips, the palette, the Inspector, Dialogs and sheets use `blur(30px) saturate(1.7)` over `popover` or `sheet`.
- **Solid.** Failure cards and warning lines (`fail-card`, `warn-card` at .97), and every signal chip and button. Solid white on the primary action, Met, toasts and badges.
- **Artwork.** The blurred ambient behind every page. A sharp, edge-masked backdrop sits behind the header of a Work page and of the portal's Work page.

### Shadow Vocabulary

The frontmatter's `shadows` block holds each shadow's value.

- **Float** (`float`): Menus, popovers, a Select's list and tooltips.
- **Float large** (`float-large`): The palette, Dialogs and the Inspector.
- **Signal lift** (`signal-lift`): Failure cards and red Signal rows. Warning lines and amber Signal rows use `warn-line` as an inset ring with no drop.
- **Poster** (`poster`): Large posters and portal poster cards.
- **Toast** (`toast`).
- **Live glow** (`live-glow`): The fill of a live bar, the current stage segment, the bar on a selected row and the live dot. The live dot adds a 3px ring at `hsl(var(--h) 90% 65% / .32)`.
- **Segment lift** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.28)`): The selected segment.
- **Focus** (`outline: 2px solid #fff; outline-offset: 2px; box-shadow: 0 0 0 6px var(--halo)`): Every focusable thing except menu items, sheet items and the palette input, which mark focus with `fill-hover` and a 2px glow underline. A selectable table row draws the outline inside its own edge, with no halo.

### Named Rules

**The Floating Glass Rule.** Blur means "this floats above the page". Nothing that scrolls with the content is blurred. That covers live pills and chips on posters too.

**The Reduce Transparency Rule.** Under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`, every glass and translucent surface turns opaque at the same tint and lightness, and the ambient image hides, leaving the tint wash. Under `prefers-contrast: more`, `ink-2` and `ink-3` rise to `ink-1`, and hairlines rise to `hairline-strong` at full opacity. Under `forced-colors: active`, surfaces drop to system colours, and every state keeps its icon and its word.

## Shapes

Controls are capsules and containers are softly squared. A control a finger or pointer presses (a button, segmented control, switch, search field, toast or pill) is a capsule at 999px. Containers step up in radius as they float higher: 6px chips, 8px menu items, inputs and small art, 14px cards, tables, menus and large posters, 20px Dialogs, and a 24px top edge on sheets. Marks smaller than 16px (state squares, stage segments, bars) round to a quarter of their height.

## Components

### Buttons

Capsules that press in.
- **Shape:** Capsule (999px). Console is 32px tall with 14px sides, and small is 28px with 12px sides. Touch is 44px with 18px sides and Body touch at 600, the primary is 50px, and a small button keeps the 44px target. A leading icon is 16px.
- **Default:** `fill` with a `hairline-strong` ring and `ink-1` text at 600. Hover turns `fill-hover`. Press scales to .97. Both transitions run 200ms on the standard ease.
- **Primary:** Solid white with `on-solid` ink and no ring. One per view.
- **Ghost:** Transparent with `ink-2`, turning `fill` and `ink-1` on hover.
- **Fail and Warn:** Solid `fail` or `warn` with deep ink, for the one fix a signal offers. Inside a failure card, the other buttons use `rgba(255,220,216,.1)` with a `.16` ring.
- **Disabled:** 40% opacity, no hover, no press.
- **Busy:** The label stays and a 14px spinner replaces the icon.
- **Icon buttons:** 32px circles in Console and 44px in Touch, with a 20px icon, `ink-2` until hovered, then `fill` and `ink-1`.

### Segmented control

An iOS-style choice of view or filter.
- **Track:** Capsule `fill` with a hairline ring and 2px padding. Options are 28px tall and 500 weight.
- **Selected:** `selected` fill with `ink-1` and the segment lift. Never solid white.
- **Counts:** Every option shows its count, All included, in tabular Geist.
- **Phone:** Full width with equal options, scrolling sideways when they overflow.
- **Semantics:** `role="radiogroup"`.

### Page tabs

The row of links under a page's title that moves between the page's sections, such as a Node's Overview, Web and API, Logs and Log settings. Each section is a route, so the row is a `nav` landmark named for the page's subject, holding links with `aria-current="page"`, never ARIA tabs.
- **Desktop:** Links are 36px tall in Body at 500, 24px apart, on a hairline that runs the row's width, with 24px below the row. A link is `ink-3`, turning `ink-1` on hover. The current link is `ink-1` with a 2px `glow` underline.
- **Phone:** No row. The page lists its sections as Touch rows with a chevron, and each section is a pushed page whose back link names the nav destination ("Nodes").

### Switch

A Console switch is 34 × 20 and a Touch switch is 51 × 31. The knob is white. The track is `hsl(var(--h) 20% 90% / .22)` off and `hsl(var(--h) 62% 46%)` on. The track fades over 250ms and the knob slides over 280ms. The whole labelled capsule is the switch target, and a sheet row with a switch is one target.

### Checkbox and radio

A checkbox is a 16px square with a 4px radius (22px and 6px in Touch). A radio is a 16px circle (22px in Touch) with a white 6px dot (8px in Touch). Both sit on `well` with a `hairline-strong` ring and fill with the switch's on colour, `hsl(var(--h) 62% 46%)`, when checked. Each sits left of its label, and the label and box together are one target at the control height.

### Fields

- **Text field:** A label in Body at 600 and `ink-2` (Body touch in Touch), then a `well` input with a hairline ring, radius 8 and 12px sides, at the input height. Touch inputs set Input touch. Focus is the standard focus ring.
- **Description and error:** Meta in `ink-3` under the input (Body touch in Touch). An error replaces the description in `fail-ink` with its 14px alert icon.
- **Read-only value:** The same input set in Data, selecting all on focus, for a value someone copies. It can carry a default Copy button beside the input, which toasts "<Label> copied". The button shows only in a secure context, because the browser's clipboard needs one, and a Node on a LAN often answers plain http.
- **PIN:** Four 40px slots (48px in Touch), 8px apart, masked and numeric.
- **Select:** A trigger shaped like the text field, with an up and down chevron in `ink-3`, opening a list styled as a menu with a check on the chosen item. An option can lead with a mark, such as a Node's status dot, and the trigger repeats the chosen option's mark. A mark that means something carries its word for a screen reader.
- **Setting field:** One setting an Admin can change: its label, its control, a Save that shows only while the draft differs, then hints in Meta (the default, the range, and "Applies after restart." for a setting the Node reads at start), and Reset to default when the value isn't the default. A refusal shows under the control in `fail-ink`. The control follows the setting's kind: a duration or count is an amount with its unit, a count whose 0 means none reads "No limit", a port is a number from 1 to 65535, a choice is a Segmented control (a Select past three options), a switch is the Switch, and a window is two times with the zone. A setting fixed by an environment variable disables its control and shows a 14px lock with "Set by" and the variable in Data, and offers no Reset. In a list, a setting can sit as a row reading "<Label>: <value>" that opens the full field in a Popover, or a sheet on phone.
- **Search field:** A `fill` capsule with a hairline ring and a 16px search icon, at the control height.

### Chips and pills

- **Chip:** 24px, radius 6, `fill`, `ink-2`, 12px at 600. A Node chip is outlined.
- **Signal chips:** Solid `fail`, `warn` or `ok` with deep ink at 700 and a 14px icon. They name a state and its stage ("Failed at Import"), and they never take the tint.
- **Live chip:** `live-fill` with a hue ring and a white glowing dot.
- **Met pill:** Solid white capsule, 28px, with a check.
- **Count:** A number in Label at `ink-3` with tabular numerals, optionally led by the live dot or a 6px amber or red dot. A screen reader hears the dot as a word.
- **Request stages (portal):** A Member sees a Request's stage (Pending, Approved, Searching, Downloading, Processing, Available or Denied) and the Delayed flag, as [Requests portal under Editions and Targets](https://github.com/jatassi/SlipStream/issues/88) decided and [ADR 0020](docs/adr/0020-a-request-stores-only-admin-decisions-and-derives-its-stage-from-the-work-tree.md) records. The Member portal's spec draws them. The Requested, Coming and Ready ribbons are retired.

### Status sentence

The first line of every page that watches live work. It reads as one sentence of counts and links ("5 jobs moving · 1 failure · relay-fra degraded"). It is set in Sentence type in the desktop shell and Sentence touch on phone, with tabular numerals, over the ambient with a soft text shadow (`0 1px 16px rgb(0 0 0 / .45)`). The live part leads with the live dot. Failure and warning parts are links to the cards below, in `fail-ink` and `warn`, each led by its signal icon. Separators are `ink-4`. A right-hand line of the day's totals shows in wide desktop windows.

### Stage track

A job's path from Searching to Available, one segment per stage (Searching, Downloading, Transfer, Import, Conversion, Available). Segments are 6px tall (8px on phone and in the Inspector) with 3px gaps.
- **Done:** Flat `ink-2`.
- **Current:** `ink-1` with the live glow.
- **Ahead:** `fill`.
- **Not needed:** A `hairline-strong` outline at 45% width.
- **Failed:** Solid `fail`.

Running tracks are `role="progressbar"` with a stage-and-title label. Others are `role="img"`.

### Progress

There are two kinds, and every bar maps to one.
- **Live.** A white fill rising from the Work's hue, with the live glow. A sheen sweeps it every 2.4s. Width moves linearly each second. The track is a 6px capsule at `hsl(var(--h) 60% 80% / .14)` with a hue ring. Used for downloads, Conversions, Transfers and live matrix cells.
- **Meter.** Flat `ink-2` on a 4px `fill` capsule, with no glow and no sheen. Used for storage, provider allowances and seeding goals.
  - **Seeding goal.** Two lanes, ratio and seeded time, 4px each in tables and 8px in the Inspector. A 2px tick marks the tracker's minimum. It turns `warn` when a hit and run is at risk, and the goal-met state carries a green chip.
  - **Allowance.** Thin by design.
- **Spinner.** One spinner: 14px, a white arc on a faint ring, turning every 0.8s.

### Failure card

The answer to "what broke". A near-opaque red-black card (`fail-card`, radius 14, signal lift). Its header row is the signal chip with the failed stage, the time in Mono `fail-dim`, and the actions on the right (the fix as a Fail button first). Its body puts the Work and its stage track beside a key and value list. The labels are Label caps in `fail-ink` (Cause, Recovery, Release), and values run to 78ch.
- **Full:** Home's lead failures and Torrents' problems, which tile at `minmax(360px, 1fr)`. The subject and its track take a third of the body and the key and value list the rest.
- **Compact:** The Work page's Needs you list. It drops the stage track, and its actions sit in a row at the bottom right.
- **Phone:** A separate 16px-radius card with its kv in one column and two actions (the fix and More).

### Signal row

The compact red or amber row for lists that can grow long: the Conversion queue's Needs you band, problems inside a table, and Needs you rows past the first few cards. It is one line of chevron, signal chip, subject (600), a two-line clamped verdict and the time, padded 12px by 16px with radius 14. On phone, the chevron, chip and time share the first line, and the subject and verdict wrap below. It expands in place to the full detail, such as each attempt with its Node, time and error in a `well`. Enter or Space toggles it, with `aria-expanded`. The chevron turns over 250ms.

### Warning line

Warnings are amber lines, never solid cards. A warning line is `warn-card` with a `warn-line` hairline, radius 14 and 12px by 20px padding, holding an amber chip, a bold subject, `warn-dim` detail, a "since" time when the warning has a start, and one Warn button. Attached under a failure card, it is that card's last strip. On its own, it is a Signal row at rest. On phone, it is a row that opens a sheet.

### Details

The approachable-first pattern. A plain-language summary leads, then a Details disclosure (a chevron and the word, 500 in `ink-2`) holds the machinery: codes, raw names and scores. The error page uses it.

### Error and not-found pages

An error stands inside the shell, so the sidebar, bars and tab bar stay and only the content shows it. It is a glass card at most 560px wide with 16px padding, leading with one plain sentence in Sentence type (Sentence touch on phone), then Try again, then Details with the code, status and detail (or a client exception's message) as a key and value list in a `well`. A page only Admins may see says so and offers Open the portal instead of Try again. A not-found page is the same card saying "There's nothing at this address." with a button to the audience's first nav row. A crash in the shell itself shows the card centred on the page with a Reload button.

### Lists and tables

Lists and tables are glass sheets with radius 14, a hairline ring and hairlines between rows. Console rows are 44px for one line and 60px for two. Touch list rows are 54px for one line and 64px for two, in iOS inset groups with 16px gutters, and their hairlines start 16px in from the left. A row's second line is Meta in `ink-3`. Table headers are 32px of Label at `ink-3`. Hover and focus wash a row at `hsl(var(--h) 50% 70% / .1)` over 350ms. A selected row adds a 3px white glowing bar on its left edge. A whole row is one target, with the arrow keys, Home and End moving between rows. A table hides a column below its own container width, and below a set width it stacks each row as a card with its labels above the values.

A list too long to draw whole, such as a Node's log, draws only the rows near its view (`VirtualRows`). It follows the tail while scrolled to the end, stops following once scrolled up, and follows again when scrolled back down.

### Edition × Target matrix and state squares

The Work page's grid of wanted cuts and versions. Every cell is in one of seven states, ranked failed, upgrade, missing, downloading, converting, Met and not aired. Each state has a chip and a square.
- **Met:** Solid white.
- **Upgrade:** A white ring with a centre dot.
- **Missing:** A hatch.
- **Live:** A fill rising from the bottom with the glow.
- **Failed:** Solid `fail`.
- **Not aired:** A hairline ring.

There are three layouts.
- **One Item.** A full table with a 168px first column. Each cell shows its state chip, its facts and its one action. Each Target header names its sourcing.
- **Many Items.** One collapsible glass section per level. The header holds the level name, a stacked state bar (180 × 8px), a tally and a level search. A section opens when anything in it is not Met or not aired. Rows are 36px, with 16px squares in 42 × 30 buttons grouped by Edition. Runs of not-aired Items fold into one line.
- **Needs you.** A list above the matrix with compact failure cards first, then upgrades and Not found rows. Consecutive identical Items merge into ranges.

Hovering or focusing a square opens a Popover with its detail, and clicking pins it. On phone, one Item becomes one card per Edition, and many Items keep their rows, with each row opening a sheet.

### Inspector

A non-modal panel on the right, for the detail of a selected row, hop or Node. It is `min(480px, 40vw)` wide with radius 20, glass with float large, held in the shell's third column 12px from the window's edge. It pushes the content left rather than covering it. It enters over 280ms from 16px right. Its header is the title in Title with a close button. Arrow keys move the selection and the Inspector follows. Its sections are wells at `hsl(var(--h) 40% 92% / .045)` with radius 14, 16px padding and a Heading. On phone, the Inspector's content is a pushed page, except a selection on a drawing (a remote access hop or a part of the Node canvas), which opens a sheet.

### Dialog

A centred modal for a decision or a guided task: provider key setup, backfill, removing torrents, Pairing and Handover. Widths are 460, 680 and 940px, with 24px padding and radius 20 on glass with float large, over a .5 black scrim. Its title is Title. It fades in from 96% scale on the spring over 360ms. Focus is trapped and returns to the opener on close. On phone, a Dialog is a bottom sheet.

### Popover and menus

Anchored to their trigger, 6px away, flipping to fit with a 12px viewport margin. A menu is at least 220px wide on glass with float, radius 14 and 4px padding. Its items are 36px with radius 8 and 16px icons, groups are split by a hairline, a toggle item carries a `glow` check, and a danger item is `fail-ink`. Detail popovers are 340px wide with 16px padding. Both enter over 180ms, fading up from 8px and 98.5% scale. Hover popovers hide 160ms after the pointer leaves, and Esc closes either kind. A popup opened from the portal keeps the Touch density. On phone, a menu is an action sheet.

A tooltip is a small label on glass with float and radius 8, in Meta at 500, fading over 180ms. The collapsed sidebar uses it for each row's label and count.

### Sheet

The phone's bottom sheet. It is glass at `sheet` with a 24px top edge, a 38 × 5 grabber and 16px sides, over a .5 black scrim. Items are 52px tall with 20px icons, grouped on `fill` with radius 14 and hairlines between them. Cancel is a separate 52px block at 600. It rises on the spring (`cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1)`, 340ms), falls over 260ms and follows a downward swipe. It stops 12px below the top safe area and scrolls when taller. Its bottom padding is 44px, or the bottom safe area plus 16px when that is larger. A sheet is always Touch.

### Command palette

⌘K on Apple platforms and Ctrl+K elsewhere toggles it, and the top bar's search capsule and the phone's Search open it. In the desktop shell it is 640px wide at 12vh from the top and at most `min(600px, 80vh)` tall, glass with float large and radius 20, over a .5 black scrim. It fades in from 98% scale over 220ms. Its 60px input row sets 17px text and turns `fill-hover` with a glow underline while focused. Results are grouped (Works, then Commands), with 44px options, each led by an 18px icon with its meta in Meta at `ink-3` on the right. Works show 5 at rest and 8 while typing. A 40px footer shows the ↑↓, ↵ and Esc hints. On phone it is full screen inside the safe areas, with 56px options and a Cancel in `glow`.

### Toast

A solid white capsule at 13px and 500, at most 440px wide, with 12px by 16px padding. It sits under the top bar at the right in the desktop shell and at the top centre on phone, under the safe area. Up to three stack, newest on top. It enters over 220ms from 8px above. A toast without an action stays 2.8s. A toast with an action carries a 28px outlined action and a dismiss button, and stays until one is used. Either kind pauses while hovered or focused, and a swipe up or right dismisses it. A failure toast leads with a fail signal chip. A toast says what happened and, when it matters, what happens next ("moved up from New. Estimated start 21:48 on forge").

### Guided steps

A numbered list with a 30px disc per step on a 1px connector.
- **Current step:** A white disc with dark ink and the Work glow.
- **Done step:** A check on `selected`.
- **Pending step:** `ink-2`.

Following a step's real link ticks it, and a pasted key is checked at once. The answer replaces the steps, either the provider's own reply in a failure card or a result card with a glowing check. Portal onboarding and provider key setup both use this pattern.

### Ranked list

A list whose order is its meaning, such as subtitle providers in search order. Each row has a Numeral rank (`ink-3`, the first in `ink-1`), a drag handle, the body with a one-sentence state and a meter, then actions with up and down buttons. Arrow keys move a row from its handle. A line above the list says what the order does.

### Route

Remote access draws each exposure as a lane of hops: Player, DNS, Relay, TLS, Node link, Origin and Media Server. A hop is a small `fill` tile joined by a 2px connector, and it takes the worst status of its checks. A failing or warning hop takes its signal card and a 3px connector. A hop past a failure shows "not reached", with a dashed outline and a dashed connector. A passing hop carries a green check. Selecting a hop opens its checks in the Inspector in the desktop shell and a sheet on phone. Each check shows what was observed, what was expected, what it means and its fix.

### Node canvas

Desktop Nodes opens on the Diagram, an isometric drawing of the install seen from one fixed camera, with no pan or zoom. It fits the page's width and sits on the page with no ground of its own. Each Node is a tile: a slab on feet with a plaque on its front edge, a state lamp and a row of Role chips. The Controller's tile is at the centre at full size, and each Worker's tile is 72% of it on a ring around it, the first eight close and the next six further out. The Diagram draws up to 15 Nodes, and a caption under it says the Roster lists the rest.

- **Tiles.** The plaque reads the Node's name and the state the Roster gives it: "Online", or "Last seen 21:31" while it is away. The lamp is green while the Node is online, amber while it reconnects or runs another version, red while it is away and the Work's glow while it restarts. An away Node's chips and machines, and a draining Role, are drawn at 55% and keep their place.
- **Machines.** A Role with a machine stands it on the tile, and only a Role the Node holds does. Downloading is an antenna that takes pieces in, a glass chute that carries them to a loom, and a board of cells for each of up to two torrents that fills as the torrent does. Storage is a cabinet of drawers, Converting a roller press and Relaying a switchboard. These three stand still until their specs give them live figures. Every other Role is a chip only.
- **Links.** Each Worker is joined to the Controller by a glass conduit on the floor, rising on a short riser under each slab. Capsules travel along it at a rate set by the link's throughput, capped by what its round trip can carry, and faster on a quicker link. The end that dialed glows. A down link is empty.
- **Lettering.** Plaques and chips are engraved in Geist Mono, as labels on equipment are. It is the one place mono sets words, because the engraving spaces every letter on one advance and is part of the drawing, not data.
- **Reading it.** Hovering a part, or the idle tour when no pointer moves, lights it and dims the rest. A click or the arrow keys select a Node, a Role or a link and open the Inspector, which says it in words with the machinery under Details. On phone the Inspector is a sheet. Escape clears.
- **Dragging.** A Role chip drags to another tile to move the Role there, or adds it while ⌥ or Alt is held. A ghost follows the pointer with the plan's word for the drop, such as "Moves Downloading". A refusal such as "seedbox already relays" doesn't name the Role, so "Add Relaying" comes before it. The tile under it tints by the plan's verdict: green allows, amber warns and red blocks. A drop opens the same Review as the Roles page. A drop on red does nothing.
- **Narrow and phone.** Below 1000px and on phone, Nodes opens on the Roster, and the Diagram tab is there to look at, without drags.
- **Motion.** Fills and capsules ease toward each traffic sample over the second until the next. Under reduced motion the Diagram is one still frame: capsules spaced along each link at its rate and every board at its fill.

The Roster says everything the Diagram draws, in rows, and a screen reader reads the Roster.

### PIN keypad

Portal sign-in and onboarding take a 4-digit PIN on a keypad of 80px circular keys in a three-column grid. A key is `hsl(var(--h) 30% 92% / .1)` with a hairline ring, and its digit is 34px Geist at 400. A press lights the key at once and fades it back over 250ms. Four 18px dots show entry. A filled dot turns `ink-1` with the Work glow and scales to 108% on the spring. A status line below counts the digits ("0 of 4"). A new PIN is entered twice, and a mismatch shakes the dots once (420ms, 9px each way) and says so in `fail-ink`.

### Emails

Emails are the one light surface, because mail clients force it. They are table layouts with no images and `color-scheme: light only`, set in Helvetica and Arial at hex and px. The page is `#f4f2ee`, the card is white with a `#e3e0da` border, and the button is a bulletproof `#1d1d1f` capsule with a VML fallback for Outlook. A callout is `#fff4dc` with `#5c3d00` text and a `#e0a020` left rule. Each email has a plain-text part built from the same blocks.

### Logo

The mark is Two Nodes: a stream S whose two ends are dots, one for each kind of Node, the Controller and the Relay. It is drawn on a 64-unit grid. The S is one stroke, `M40 14H25a9 9 0 0 0 0 18h14a9 9 0 0 1 0 18H24`, 8 units wide with round caps and joins. The dots are 5.5-unit circles at (52, 14) and (12, 50). The masters live in `brand/`.

- **In the app.** The mark is inline SVG. The S takes `ink-1` and the dots take `glow`, so the dots follow the Work in focus like every other light on the page.
- **Outside the app.** The favicon, the tray icon, the installer and anything else SlipStream cannot tint use the neutral resolution in `brand/mark.svg` (`#f2f4f7` and `#74affb`). `brand/app-icon.svg` puts that mark at 70% on a `page` tile with a 14-unit radius. The web app links `brand/mark.svg` as its favicon. `go run ./cmd/release gen icons` renders `brand/app-icon.svg` to the 180px Apple touch icon and the web manifest's 192px and 512px icons, and `release lint` checks them against `brand/icons.sha256`. The manifest's colours and the `theme-color` meta are `page`'s neutral, `#0c0d0e`.
- **On light ground.** Emails and the light Windows taskbar take `brand/mark-light.svg`, with the S in `#1d1d1f`, the email button's ink, and the dots in `#1464cc`.
- **Wordmark.** The mark sits left of "SlipStream" in Geist, "Slip" at 600 and "Stream" at 400, with -0.03em tracking. The mark's 64-unit box is twice the cap height, and the gap between them is a third of that box.
- **Size and space.** The smallest size is 16px, where the stroke lands on 2px. Keep clear space of one dot's width on every side.
- **Startup sequence.** The app opens on a 4.4s sequence built from the mark. The two dots come online over a faint dot grid, and the Controller sends a Transfer that draws the S behind it. The Relay answers, the neutral ambient blooms, and the mark slides left as the wordmark wipes out from behind it. It uses the neutral blue, because no Work is in focus yet.
  - **Master.** `brand/startup/sequence.js` is the master. It exports the duration, the phase table and `render(t, stage)`. The web app imports it, so the app and the master cannot drift, and it draws with the bundled Geist and `brand/startup/grain.png` from the Node. `brand/startup/startup.html` plays the same module on its own, and `brand/startup/render.mjs` renders `startup.mp4` from that page frame by frame.
  - **When it plays.** Once per new tab or app window, on whichever page opens first: sign-in, setup, the app or the portal. It is a full-screen layer over the page, which loads underneath it. Any key, click or tap skips to the finished lockup. It never plays while the Startup sequence switch is off, a per-browser choice in the account menu and in More.
  - **How it ends.** In the Admin app's desktop shell it stops as its exit begins (3.96s), and its mark settles into the sidebar's mark through a 360ms view transition. Everywhere else it plays its full 4.4s and fades over 360ms. Under reduced motion it shows the finished lockup for 0.6s, then fades.
- **Restarting.** While the Node the browser talks to restarts, a near-opaque `page` layer covers the shell and the startup sequence's mark returns, still and at 48px, over "Restarting SlipStream…" in Sentence and a Spinner. The page asks the Node every second and reloads once a later start answers. A restart onto a new port says "SlipStream is moving to port 9000" instead, with a primary link to the new port only when the browser reached the Node on its own port. After 90s with no answer the layer holds the error card, "SlipStream hasn't come back.", with Try again and the log folder under Details. The connection line stays quiet throughout. A Worker restarting shows no layer: its sections read "<name> is restarting…" with a Spinner, and the Node picker gives it a glowing dot until its link is up again.

**The Two Dots Rule.** The dots are always the Work's glow or the neutral blue. They never take a signal colour, and the mark never stands in for a status.

### Motion

- **Easing.** The standard ease is `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`, and the spring is `cubic-bezier(.32, .72, 0, 1)`.
- **Ambient.** The tint (`--h`, `--s`) and the backdrop crossfade over 1.1s on the standard ease. The incoming backdrop settles from 104% scale over 1.6s.
- **Controls.** Buttons, segments and switches respond in 200 to 280ms. Disclosure chevrons rotate over 250ms. Sections open and close at once, with no height animation. The phone nav bar and the portal's top nav turn to glass over 200ms.
- **Overlays.** Menus and tooltips take 180ms, the palette and toasts 220ms, the Inspector 280ms, sheets 340ms in and 260ms out, and Dialogs 360ms.
- **Shell.** The sidebar collapses and expands over 280ms on the standard ease. A page change in the desktop shell crossfades the content alone over 180ms. On phone, a push slides the new page in from the right over 340ms on the spring while the old page drifts 30% left and dims to 60%, and a pop reverses it. The bars, the sidebar and the tab bar hold still through both. In an installed web app, a drag of 80px or more from the screen's left 20px goes back.
- **Live.** Fills move linearly once a second, and the sheen loops every 2.4s. The spinner turns every 0.8s.
- **Startup.** The sequence runs 4.4s and leaves over 360ms, as the Logo section says.

The frontmatter's `motion` block holds these easings and the overlay durations. `release gen tokens` writes them to the theme as `--ease-*` and `--duration-*`.

**The Reduce Motion Rule.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the tint and backdrop swap at once, fills jump without the sheen, and overlays appear without travel. Page transitions and the sidebar cut. Spinners, steps, the PIN shake and canvas animations stop. No animation is exempt.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every surface and ink from the formulas in Colors, so a Work's hue reaches the whole page.
- **Do** hold the ambient at or below relative luminance 0.053, measured per backdrop.
- **Do** run `go run ./cmd/release lint` after changing any colour token, and keep its contrast check passing.
- **Do** lead every watching page with a status sentence, then failures with their stage, cause and recovery, then live work.
- **Do** show live work as white with the Work's glow, and Met as solid white.
- **Do** pair every signal colour with an icon and a word.
- **Do** use capsules for every control, at 32px in Console and 44px or more in Touch.
- **Do** blur only what floats: bars, menus, popovers, the palette, the Inspector, Dialogs and sheets.
- **Do** set rates, sizes, ETAs, timestamps, release names, paths and keys in Geist Mono, and everything else in Geist.
- **Do** use the neutral ambient (214 / 14%) on every screen without a Work.
- **Do** pad bars and sheets with `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** draw a light theme. v2 has one dark theme (parity row P-012 is excepted). Emails are the only light surface.
- **Don't** tint red, amber or green, and don't let a failed Work drive the ambient.
- **Don't** use green for Met, for live work or for a solid card. Green marks a passed check or a healthy thing, as a chip, dot or icon.
- **Don't** add a fourth red. `fail-ink` covers failure text on glass, including the status sentence's link.
- **Don't** set a name, a sentence, a count in prose or a step number in Geist Mono.
- **Don't** use solid white for a selected segment or tab. Solid white means the primary action or Met.
- **Don't** blur anything that scrolls with the content, such as a live pill or a chip on a poster.
- **Don't** put text over a sharp backdrop unless a mask or scrim holds the ambient ceiling beneath it.
- **Don't** use `ink-4` for text.
- **Don't** use uppercase outside a failure card's key labels.
- **Don't** use B's condensed display face (Big Shoulders Display). Geist sets the portal too.
- **Don't** show Members any machinery: no Nodes, Targets, stages, releases or Conversions on the portal.
